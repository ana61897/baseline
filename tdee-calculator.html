  • Sedentary means a desk job with little to no structured exercise. If you're mostly sitting all day and getting under roughly 5,000 steps, you're still sedentary, even if you hit the gym once in a while.
  • Light covers structured exercise 1–3 days a week (think jogging, lifting, or a spin class), plus a fairly still day otherwise. Usually lands around 5,000–7,500 steps a day.
  • Moderate means exercise 3–5 days a week, or a job that keeps you on your feet (teaching, retail, nursing). This is usually where 7,500–10,000 steps a day falls.
  • Active means hard exercise 6–7 days a week, or a physically demanding job on top of regular training. Typically 10,000–12,500 steps a day.
  • Very Active is for two-a-day training, competitive athletes, or physically taxing work (construction, farming) combined with intentional exercise. Usually 12,500+ steps a day.

A Few Things Worth Knowing

No fads or fear-mongering, just what the research actually says.

🥩

No, You Don't Need That Much Protein

As much as Big Protein™ would like you to believe you need 150 grams a day to stop your muscles and bones from disintegrating, the research does not support it. Studies suggest that protein intake above 1.6g/kg of body weight provides little to no additional muscle-building benefit. For maintaining muscle while eating in a calorie deficit, around 1.2g/kg is the recommended target — that's the calculation reflected in our macro calculator. See the study.

🌾

Fiber Is Essential

Fiber aids digestion, keeps you full for longer, and feeds the healthy bacteria in your gut that support your immune system. With colorectal cancer rates climbing among younger adults in recent years, getting enough fiber has never mattered more.

🍠

Carbs Are Friends

Carbs are your body's main fuel source. They support brain function, protect your muscle tissue, and power your organs. Whole grains, beans, rice, and potatoes are a great part of a healthy diet — in moderation.
